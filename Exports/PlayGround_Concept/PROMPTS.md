# PlayGround remake: concept prompts (2026-11-16)

Pictures of today's PlayGround are in `screenshots/` (`PlayGround_now_sheet.jpg` = 4 of them on one page).
Taken in a test run with the walls down and the countdown on. The fog was switched off for these pictures only;
in the game the house fog hides everything further than about 22 m.

**How to use (Gemini or ChatGPT):** start a new chat and attach `PlayGround_now_sheet.jpg` (plus 1-2 single
screenshots if you like). Paste ONE prompt below. Make the overview first. When you like one, ask for the eye-level
view "of this same design", then the prop sheet. Prompt 1's first line tells the model what the attached pictures are.

---

## Prompt 1: the whole PlayGround from above (key art)

```text
The attached screenshots are today's version of a level in my VR game. Use them only for the colour palette, the
centrepiece (a witch's cauldron with green fire on a stone fire ring) and the overall size; redesign everything else
to be much richer.

Concept art for a VR action-game arena: "The Slime Orchard Playground", a haunted children's playground on Halloween
night. High three-quarter bird's-eye view of the whole arena, about 50 x 50 metres of play area, with a ring of
spooky trees and low hills around it so it feels enclosed without hard walls.

In the exact centre: a big witch's cauldron bubbling bright green on a ring of stones with a small campfire under it
(the thing the player defends). Around it, in loose rings and clusters with open lanes between them for fighting
and running:
- a broken haunted playground: rusty swing set with one swing still moving, a twisted slide, a seesaw shaped like a
  bone, a candy-cane monkey-bars dome, a merry-go-round with pumpkin seats, a sandbox full of candy sand;
- giant toys, half sunk in the ground: a wooden rocking horse with a skeleton grin, toy building blocks with carved
  Halloween faces, a jack-in-the-box, wind-up tin robots, a spinning top, a deflated beach ball, toy soldiers;
- trees with chunky glossy jelly-bubble crowns (pink, aqua, lilac, teal) on twisted plum "licorice" trunks with
  diagonal stripes, a few lantern trees hung with small glowing jack-o'-lanterns, and dead bare trees at the edge;
- mossy boulders with dripping slime, candy-crystal rocks (geodes with glowing pastel crystals), cracked
  gravestones, stepping stones in the grass;
- statues: a stone knight holding a pumpkin, a weeping gargoyle cat, a jack-o'-lantern totem pole, a cracked
  angel with candy wings, a giant skull with glowing eyes;
- lots of small breakable things scattered everywhere: crates, barrels, candy baskets, clay pots, pumpkins of all
  sizes, lollipop signposts, picket-fence pieces, lanterns.
Four spooky gates or holes at the edges of the arena where monsters come in (glowing purple mist).

Style: stylised low-poly game art, chunky readable shapes, flat colours with soft painted gradients, a little gloss
on the jelly and slime parts, like a polished indie VR game. No photo realism, no fine noisy detail.
Palette: deep indigo-violet night sky with a big orange harvest moon, warm orange horizon glow, teal moss ground with
small glowing goo puddles, plum-grey stone, pastel candy accents, green cauldron light, orange jack-o'-lantern
glows. Cosy-creepy, fun, not gory. No text, no logos, no UI.
```

## Prompt 2: the same design at eye level (what the player sees in VR)

```text
Same PlayGround design as before, now seen from the player's eyes: standing on the moss ground 1.6 m tall, about 8 m
from the bubbling green cauldron, looking across the arena toward the orange harvest moon. Wide field of view like a
VR headset. Show the scale: swing set and giant toys taller than the player, breakable crates and pumpkins at knee
and waist height, jelly-bubble trees towering overhead. A few small flying slimes with bat wings circle the moon.
Same stylised low-poly look and palette, flat colours with soft gradients, glowing accents, no text.
```

## Prompt 3: the props as a model sheet (for modelling)

```text
Game prop concept sheet, stylised low-poly, for a Halloween VR action game "Slime Orchard Playground". A neat grid of
24 separate props on a plain dark grey background, each shown at three-quarter view with a little space around it,
same style and lighting for all. Chunky readable silhouettes, flat colours with soft painted gradients, a little gloss
on jelly and slime, simple shapes that would work as under 2,000 triangles each. Palette: plum, teal moss, pastel
pink/aqua/lilac candy, orange pumpkin glow, slime green.
Row 1, trees: jelly-bubble crown tree with twisted striped licorice trunk; lantern tree with hanging jack-o'-lanterns;
mushroom tree; dead bare spooky tree; small jelly bush; slime-covered stump.
Row 2, rocks and statues: mossy boulder dripping slime; candy-crystal geode rock; cracked gravestone; stepping
stones; stone knight statue holding a pumpkin; jack-o'-lantern totem pole.
Row 3, playground and toys: broken swing set; bone-shaped seesaw; rocking horse with a skeleton grin; jack-in-the-box;
toy block with a carved face; wind-up tin robot.
Row 4, small breakables: wooden crate; barrel; candy basket; clay pot; stack of pumpkins; lollipop signpost.
Each breakable also shown in a small version broken into 3-5 chunky pieces next to it.
No text, no labels, no logos.
```

---

**Tips**
- If it comes out too dark: add "brighter, readable, more moonlight and glow".
- If it's too busy: "fewer, bigger props, more open space between clusters".
- For a single prop: "make the [rocking horse] alone, front, side and back views, same style".
- Keep it buildable for Quest: chunky shapes, no thin wires or leaves, few materials (one shared texture).
