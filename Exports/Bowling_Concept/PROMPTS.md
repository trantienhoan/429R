# Gate 2 (Slime Bowling) remake: concept prompts (2026-11-18)

`Bowling_now_sheet.jpg` = today's gate 2 (brightened a little so it reads; the originals are in `screenshots/`):
- top row: the whole arena from above (the X of four lanes, the slime over a deck), and the player's view from the
  dirt island down a lane (the rules sign, the ball sprouts at the feet);
- middle row: the Giant Flying Slime dropping a rack of slime pins onto a deck, and a 6-pin rack between the two fire
  braziers;
- bottom row: looking back from a pin deck at the island, and the finale: the giant jack-o'-lantern ball, dizzy at the
  dirt's edge after bowling itself down a lane.

`screenshots/` also has a top-down view (`b_top_down.png`), a low side view (`g_side_low.png`), the finale from above
(`i_pumpkin_overview.png`) and the slime from the island (`d_slime_from_island.png`). Taken in a test run (phase 1,
then the finale forced). The fog was switched off for these pictures only; in the game it starts at 14 m.

**What today's version lacks:** the arena is four bare dark planks and a dirt square floating in an empty dark void.
Nothing tells you where you are, the lanes have no detail, the decks are just a ring and two fires, and most of the
picture is black.

**How to use (Gemini or ChatGPT):** start a new chat, attach `Bowling_now_sheet.jpg`, paste Prompt 1. When you like a
design, ask for Prompt 2, 3 and 4 "of this same design". The look is the soft toy-diorama style you liked on the
rooftop concept (see `../Rooftop_Concept/TEXTURE_STYLE.md`).

What has to stay (the game needs it):
- a small flat island in the exact middle, about 5 x 5 m, where the player stands (the only place they can teleport to):
  4 vines grow the pumpkin bowling balls there, and the start chest and the seed drop there;
- 4 straight, flat bowling lanes in an X (90 degrees apart), about 2 m wide and 20 m long, level with the island, with
  low rails or gutters only: nothing over the lanes lower than about 3 m (the giant pumpkin rolls down them);
- at the far end of each lane a pin deck with room for a 6-pin triangle rack (pins about 0.5 m apart) and a light on
  each side (today: fire braziers);
- open sky over the lanes: the Giant Flying Slime (a fat green pumpkin-slime with bat wings) flies above and drops its
  slime pins onto the decks;
- empty space round the lanes (falling off costs health), so no floors between the lanes;
- room for the rules sign (pictures only) at the start of one lane;
- the Halloween dusk sky of the main game: deep indigo top, violet, a warm orange glow low down, a big harvest moon,
  flying slime silhouettes.

## Prompt 1: the whole arena from above (key art)

```text
The attached screenshots are today's version of a level in my VR game. Keep its layout (a small flat island in the
middle with four long straight bowling lanes in an X, each ending in a pin deck, all floating high in the sky) and its
cast (a fat green flying slime with bat wings that drops green slime bowling pins, and pumpkin bowling balls), but
redesign the look to be much richer and more magical.

Concept art for a VR action-game arena: "The Slime Bowl, a witch's bowling alley above the clouds". High three-quarter
bird's-eye view at dusk. In the middle floats a small round island of earth and rock (about 5 m across) with roots and
crystals hanging under it: a cosy pumpkin patch with four curling vines growing round orange pumpkin bowling balls, a
ball-return trough shaped like a bubbling cauldron, a few candle lanterns, an old treasure chest.

From the island, four long straight bowling lanes reach out in an X like wooden bridges in the sky: polished honey-
coloured wood with inlaid gold arrow markers and dark gutters, low carved rails, small hanging lanterns, held up by
chains to little floating rocks and by crooked wooden trestles that fade into the clouds below. Nothing arches over
the lanes.

At the far end of each lane, a round stone pin deck like a little stage: a glowing gold rune ring holds a triangle of
six green slime pins with goofy carved faces, two big iron fire braziers on either side, a crooked pumpkin-topped arch
BEHIND the deck (not over the lane) with a curtain of purple mist. Each deck has its own colour of rune glow.

Above it all, a fat round green slime with a pumpkin stem and small bat wings flies over one lane, dripping slime and
dropping a fresh rack of pins. Far below, a sea of soft purple clouds; in the distance more floating islands with tiny
crooked pumpkin houses, and the big orange harvest moon with flying slime silhouettes crossing it.

Style: stylised low-poly game art like a soft toy diorama: chunky shapes with bevelled rounded edges, flat colours
with soft painted gradients, almost no texture noise, soft dark contact shadows where things meet, polished indie VR
game, not photo-real. Palette: deep indigo-violet sky with a warm orange glow low down, honey wood, plum-grey stone,
saturated pumpkin orange, slime green, gold runes, purple magic. Warm firelight pools against cool moonlight.
Cosy-creepy, not gory. No text, no logos, no UI, no numbers.
```

## Prompt 2: the player's view from the island (mid-game)

```text
Same Slime Bowl design as before, now from the player's eyes (1.6 m tall) standing on the small middle island, looking
straight down one long lane toward its pin deck. At the player's feet: curling green vines with pumpkin bowling balls
growing on them, a few fallen autumn leaves, the cauldron ball-return. A pumpkin ball is rolling fast down the lane,
leaving a trail of sparkles, toward a rack of six green slime pins in a glowing gold ring between two big fire
braziers. Overhead, the fat green bat-winged slime hovers above the deck, wobbling as it drops the next pins. The other
three lanes reach away to the sides like bridges into the sky. Big harvest moon, purple clouds below. Wide VR field of
view. Same soft toy-diorama low-poly look, no text.
```

## Prompt 3: the finale, the giant pumpkin bowls at you

```text
Same Slime Bowl design, the finale, from the player's eyes on the middle island. The slimes are gone. A giant carved
jack-o'-lantern ball, a little wider than the lane, with an evil grin and glowing yellow eyes, is rolling straight
down the lane toward the island, throwing up puffs of smoke and sparks, its face turning over and over as it rolls.
The lane lanterns swing as it passes, the braziers at the far deck flare, cracked pin pieces and slime splashes are
left behind it. Dramatic orange moonlight and fire glow against the purple sky. Exciting but fun, not gory. Same soft
toy-diorama low-poly look, no text.
```

Game prop concept sheet, soft toy-diorama low-poly style, Halloween VR action game, "The Slime Bowl, a witch's bowling

Row 1: straight wooden lane piece with gold arrow inlays and gutters; lane rail with a small hanging lantern; wooden

**Tips**
- Too dark: "brighter, more warm firelight and lantern glow on the lanes". Too busy: "fewer, bigger props, keep the
  lanes clean".
- The lanes must stay STRAIGHT, FLAT and OPEN above; if the model adds arches or roofs over a lane, say "nothing over
  the lanes, keep them open to the sky".
- Keep the island small (one player's room-scale space). If it grows into a big plaza, say "the middle island is only
  about 5 m across".
- If the lanes come out curved or tilted: "four straight lanes in a perfect X, level with the island".
