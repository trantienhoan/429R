same story props come back in every room, each time stronger (see "The rooms after this one" at the end).

## What has to stay (the game needs it)

- One square room, about 9 x 9 m, ceiling about 3 m, flat wooden floor (teleport), plum-purple damask wallpaper above
  dark wainscoting (the walls sink into the floor when the fight starts, so wall things go with them).
- An open middle for the fights, with the round rune circle on the floor (pumpkins and the room's monsters appear
  there). Furniture and clutter only along the walls and in the corners.
- North: the wooden door with a horseshoe (it breaks: a coin-toss shortcut). South: one small window.
- The witch-hat table (3 magic hats), the wardrobe, tall shelves, a small table with two chairs and a candle, the clover
  banner, one painting on the east wall.
- Lots of breakables: about 100 cardboard boxes, pumpkins, a broom, wooden crates.
- Hiding places behind the boxes: the laughing pumpkin hides and runs there.

## The story to show (the room tells it, no words)

1. **The portrait** (it replaces the grumpy-pumpkin painting): in a gilded frame, the evil pumpkin, still small, young
   and sly, holds a glass jar with the tiny green fairy glowing weakly inside. The same portrait hangs in every lair;
   from room to room the pumpkin in it is bigger and the fairy's light weaker.
2. **Green jars** on the shelves among the boxes: mason jars of glowing green "drained light" with cork lids, some
   empty and cracked. A few little pumpkins sit huddled round one jar, as if drinking its glow.
3. **The laughing pumpkin's nest** in one corner behind the boxes: straw, candy wrappers, pumpkin seeds, a tiny green
   glow in the middle (it holds its piece of the Green Seed there).
4. **The bugs are coming** (only a little yet): cobwebs in the corners and between shelf posts; a few pumpkin-orange
   spider egg sacs with a faint green glow inside; small bug holes in the floorboards and the wainscoting with claw
   scratches round them; chewed cardboard boxes spilling candy; one thin pumpkin vine with curly tendrils creeping up a
   wall from a crack in the floor.
5. **The specimen board**: a framed board of pinned spiders turning, in four steps, from a normal black spider into a
   half-pumpkin spider (orange ribbed body, olive legs, glowing eyes): the evil pumpkin's power changes the bugs.
6. **Lair marks**: claw scratches on the door, the clover banner torn and nibbled at its edges.

## Building it later (notes for me)

- Props under 2,000 triangles, on shared atlases, bevelled; holes, scratches and stains as flat decal quads.
- Glows by emission plus bloom, no real lights (Quest); the jars can use the seed's green glow particles.
- Nothing in the open middle (fights, teleport); wall props go on the kit walls so they sink with them.
- Room kits: the storage room is the Bedroom kit's "Mini" set (`Assets/Prefabs/Bases/Bedroom_Kit_Base.prefab`).

**Tips**
- Too dark: "brighter, more warm candle light and moonlight". Too busy: "fewer, bigger props, more open floor".
- Too gory or too scary for the style: "creepy but fun, cartoon toy style".
- If Gemini fills the middle: "keep the middle of the floor empty around the rune circle, props only along the walls".
- If the pumpkins or bugs drift from the game's style: "match the pumpkins and bugs in the second image exactly".
