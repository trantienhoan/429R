# Rooftop remake: concept prompts (2026-11-16)

`Rooftop_now_sheet.jpg` = today's rooftop:
- top row: the tutorial (overview, and what you see at the start);
- middle row: the tree defence (overview, and a gate arch with the tree behind it);
- bottom row: a monster gate up close, and the courtyard seen through the bars of a gate.

The separate pictures are in `screenshots/`. The fog was switched off for these pictures only.

**How to use in Gemini:** attach `Rooftop_now_sheet.jpg`, paste Prompt 1 (the whole rooftop). When you like a
design, ask for Prompt 2 and 3 "of this same design". Prompt 4 is a prop sheet for modelling.

What has to stay (the game needs it):
- the cross shape, flat and walkable (teleport);
- the round stage in the middle where the Pumpkin Tree grows (and the final boss bursts out later);
- 4 monster gates that open during the tree defence;
- the long start arm for the tutorial;
- room for the Tip Knight, the Fairy, the 3 gate dice, the whack barrels and candy baskets;
- no walls in the middle (the fights need room).

## Prompt 1: the whole rooftop from above (key art)

```text
The attached screenshots are today's version of a level in my VR game. Keep its layout (a cross-shaped rooftop with
four arms, a round stage in the middle, a long start arm) and its centrepiece (a magic pumpkin tree), but redesign
the look to be much richer and more atmospheric.

Concept art for a VR action-game arena: "The Rooftop of the Haunted House". High three-quarter bird's-eye view at
night. A flat, cross-shaped stone rooftop about 30 x 50 metres sits on top of a giant crooked haunted house. Beyond
the low battlements you can see the house's steep slate roof slopes, crooked chimney stacks with purple smoke, glowing
attic windows, a rusty weather vane shaped like a witch, stone gargoyles on the corners, and far below a tiny sleeping
Halloween village with orange windows.

In the exact middle: a round stone stage carved with a glowing gold rune circle, and on it a magic Pumpkin Tree: a
thick gnarled trunk with a big glowing carved jack-o'-lantern face, roots spreading over the stones, a crown of
golden-orange autumn leaves with small glowing pumpkins hanging like fruit. Around the stage: four wooden barrels
standing on their own small gold rune circles, clusters of pumpkins, candy baskets, lamp posts with candle lanterns.

At the end of each of the four arms (or in the inner corners): a monster gate, a round stone arch with iron bars and a
carved stone jack-o'-lantern on top, red magic warning runes glowing on the bars, purple mist leaking out from behind.

The long south arm is the start: a calm walkway with glowing stepping stones, two big wooden signposts, a small knight
NPC waiting by a lantern, and three carved stone dice on pedestals glowing different colours (the gates to three
paths).

Style: stylised low-poly game art, chunky readable shapes, flat colours with soft painted gradients, polished indie VR
game, not photo-real. Palette: deep indigo-violet night sky with a big orange harvest moon, a warm orange glow on the
horizon, cool plum-grey stone, warm orange pumpkin and candle light, gold runes, purple magic. Cosy-creepy, not gory.
No text, no logos, no UI.
```

## Prompt 2: the tutorial, at eye level

```text
Same rooftop design as before, now from the player's eyes (1.6 m tall) at the start of the long south arm, looking
toward the middle. Calm and welcoming: glowing stepping stones lead to the round stage, where a tiny fairy is trapped
in a slowly spinning tower of purple light. A small knight in a green cloak waits by a lantern on the right. Two wooden
signposts with simple glowing pictures of a controller and an arrow (no words). The three carved stone dice glow on
their pedestals. Lamp posts, pumpkins, the haunted house's chimneys and the big orange moon behind. Wide VR field of
view. Same stylised low-poly look, no text.
```

## Prompt 3: the tree defence, at eye level (mid-battle)

```text
Same rooftop design, now mid-battle, from the player's eyes on the round stage next to the Pumpkin Tree. The tree is
fully grown and glowing, its jack-o'-lantern face angry, golden leaves falling. Two of the four monster gates have
burst open: purple mist pours out with a swarm of bugs (big cartoon spiders, cockroaches, and fat bees) running and
flying toward the tree. Candy and pumpkin chunks lie scattered on the stones, a barrel is smashed. Dramatic orange
moonlight, purple gate glow, warm tree glow. Exciting but fun, not gory. Same stylised low-poly look, no text.
```

## Prompt 4: rooftop props as a model sheet

```text
Game prop concept sheet, stylised low-poly, Halloween VR action game, "Rooftop of the Haunted House". A neat grid of 20
separate props on a plain dark grey background, three-quarter view, same lighting, chunky silhouettes, flat colours
with soft painted gradients, each simple enough for under 2,000 triangles. Palette: plum-grey stone, rusty iron,
pumpkin orange, gold runes, purple magic glow.
Row 1: monster gate arch with iron bars and a stone jack-o'-lantern on top (closed); the same gate burst open and
broken; battlement wall piece; corner tower piece with a gargoyle.
Row 2: crooked chimney stack; witch weather vane; slate roof slope piece; glowing attic window.
Row 3: lamp post with a candle lantern; wooden signpost; carved stone die on a pedestal (3 colour variants); round stone
stage with a gold rune circle.
Row 4: barrel on a small rune circle; candy basket; cluster of pumpkins; candle on a stone block; broken stone rubble
pile; the Pumpkin Tree (small, medium and fully grown).
No text, no labels, no logos.
```

**Tips**
- Too dark: "brighter, more moonlight and warm candle glow". Too busy: "fewer, bigger props, more open stone floor".
- The rooftop must stay FLAT and open in the middle; if Gemini adds stairs or walls in the middle, say "no stairs or
  walls on the walkable cross, keep it one flat level".
