## Prompt 1: key art, bird's-eye
```text
The attached image is my current concept for a level in my VR game. Keep its style, colours, mood and content
(the haunted-house rooftop at night, the glowing Pumpkin Tree, monster gates, lanterns, pumpkins, barrels, candy
baskets, the big orange moon), but rebuild the rooftop's SHAPE out of giant stone cubes.

Concept art for a VR action-game arena: "The Rooftop of Cubes". High three-quarter bird's-eye view at night.
The walkable rooftop is made of huge carved stone cubes, each about 8 x 8 metres wide (as big as a small house),
set edge to edge with flat tops, forming a plus/cross shape:
- THE MIDDLE: 4 big cubes in a 2 x 2 square form the central plaza. Exactly where their 4 corners meet stands the
  magic Pumpkin Tree on a round two-step stone dais with a glowing gold rune circle: a thick gnarled trunk with a big
  glowing carved jack-o'-lantern face, roots spreading across the four cubes, a chunky crown of golden-orange leaves
  with small glowing pumpkins hanging in it.
- THE 4 PATHS: from each side of the plaza, a path of cubes leads out. Three paths are 1 cube long; the start path
  (toward the viewer) is 2 cubes long. At the end of each path stands a monster gate: a round stone arch with iron
  bars glowing with red runes, a carved stone jack-o'-lantern on top, purple mist leaking out.
- The cubes are visibly separate blocks: clear seams and slightly uneven heights where they meet (a step of a few
  centimetres), chunky bevelled edges catching the moonlight, low crenellated battlements only along the outer edges.
  Each cube's sides drop away into darkness; some cubes look slightly shifted or tilted, as if the rooftop was
  assembled by magic. Small stone rubble and a few loose smaller cubes at the corners.
- The start path: glowing cream stepping stones, two wooden signposts, a small knight in green armour by a lantern,
  three carved stone dice on pedestals glowing green, red and blue, stacks of wooden crates, barrels and a pumpkin patch.
- Around the plaza: wooden barrels on small glowing gold rune circles, candy baskets, piles of pumpkins, crooked lamp
  posts with hanging candle lanterns.
- Around and below the cubes: the haunted house's steep slate roofs with glowing attic windows, crooked chimneys with
  purple smoke, stone gargoyles, a witch weather vane, a tiny sleeping village far below with orange windows.

Style: stylised low-poly game art like a soft toy diorama: chunky shapes with bevelled rounded edges, flat colours
with soft painted gradients, almost no texture noise, soft dark contact shadows where things meet, polished indie VR
game, not photo-real. Palette: deep indigo-violet night sky with a big orange harvest moon, cool plum-grey stone,
warm orange pumpkin and candle light, gold runes, purple magic. Cosy-creepy, not gory. No text, no logos, no UI.
```
