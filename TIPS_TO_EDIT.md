# Every tip in the game, and where to change it

There are two places with tips.

## 1. The Knight's tip board (33 tips)

**Where:** `Assets/Prefabs/Player/Knight.prefab`, component **Tip Board**.

**How to edit:**
1. In the Project window, double-click `Assets/Prefabs/Player/Knight.prefab` to open it.
2. In the Hierarchy search box, type `t:TipBoard` and click the object that shows up.
3. In the Inspector, under **Tip Board**, you'll find:
   - **First Tip** and **First Tip Hint**: shown first, every time the game starts.
   - **Tips**: one is picked at random after that. A hard hit on the board shows the next one.
   - **Hints**: Hint 1 goes with Tip 1, Hint 2 with Tip 2, and so on. A hint stays hidden until the player touches the board. Leave a hint empty for no hint.
4. Press Ctrl+S to save the prefab.

Running *Tools > 429 Game > Menu > Set Up Knight Tip Board* again won't overwrite your edits.

In the tables below, **Now** is what the game shows today. **Funny idea** is only a suggestion; use it, change it, or ignore it. A short tip with the "how" hidden in the hint lets players figure things out.

**Where:** the scene `2 Game Scene`, object **Pause Menu**, component **Pause Menu**, list **Tips**. One is picked at random every time the menu opens.

**How to edit:** click **Pause Menu** in the Hierarchy, edit **Tips** in the Inspector, then save the scene (Ctrl+S).

| 4 | Trade your candy wrap for weapons in the Candy Shop. | Wraps buy weapons. Spend them before you die! |
